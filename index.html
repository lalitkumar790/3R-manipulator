Motor torque feasibility

ESTIMATED

Based on CAD-derived solid PLA masses, assumed 32 g downstream motors and 18 g pen. Conservative straight-arm pen-drag direction. This is a model estimate, not a motor test.

Calculated against a 34.3 mN·m reference at 120 Hz; no friction margin included.

At 0.10 N contact force, roughly 0.028 N·m of the base-joint demand comes from pen drag alone. A 5 V ULN2003 Darlington driver has voltage drop; actual motor torque can be lower. Motor temperature, supply voltage, backlash and bearing friction are unmeasured.

Hindi and drawing limitations

Hindi uses Devanagari font outlines (not human handwriting centerlines). Multi-stroke paths are kept in drawing order. Exact appearance depends on the available font. Physical Arduino writing still requires calibrated hardware and a real pen-lift actuator.

Geometry and physics scope

The 3D viewer automatically renders decimated triangle meshes generated from the five supplied STLs. No CAD upload is needed. Joint axes, motor couplings, and contact parameters still require physical calibration.
